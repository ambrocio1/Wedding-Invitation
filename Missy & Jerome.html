Our love story

Eight years, two cities, one umbrella

We met in a university library in 2019, quietly arguing over the last copy of a book neither of us finished. Coffee became dinners, dinners became a long-distance chapter between Singapore and Melbourne, and every reunion felt like coming home.

Last December, under a borrowed umbrella beside the swan lake, Jerome asked and Missy said yes before he could finish the question.
